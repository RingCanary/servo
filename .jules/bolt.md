## 2024-05-24 - [O(N^2) Vector Deduplication Pattern]
**Learning:** In WebIDL implementations for collections like `HTMLCollection`, `HTMLFormElement`, and `NamedNodeMap`, deduplicating property names frequently relied on O(N) lookup operations like `Vec::contains()` or `iter().any()`, leading to overall O(N^2) complexity. In a codebase parsing high-node-count documents, this scales poorly. Moreover, `DOMString::from()` allocates heap memory; doing so *before* verifying uniqueness causes massive allocation churn.
**Action:** When deduplicating elements in DOM collections, ALWAYS use a `std::collections::HashSet` to track seen items (`Atom`s or `&str`s). Defer allocating `DOMString` or performing complex string operations until *after* the `HashSet::insert()` call confirms the item is unique.
