## 2024-05-18 - [Optimized SupportedPropertyNames deduplication]
 **Learning:** In Servo's DOM collection classes (`HTMLCollection`, `HTMLFormElement`, `NamedNodeMap`), `SupportedPropertyNames` previously relied on `.contains()` or `.iter().any()` on `Vec<DOMString>` to ensure uniqueness of exposed property names. This is an `O(N^2)` operation that scales poorly with many elements/attributes and eagerly allocated heap strings. `Atom`s (from `stylo_atoms`) in Servo represent interned strings and are extremely cheap to clone, making them ideal keys for `HashSet` deduplication prior to `DOMString` conversion.
 **Action:** Always use `std::collections::HashSet` with interned strings (`Atom`s) or string slices (`&str`) to perform `O(1)` deduplication lookups during collection mapping, reducing overall complexity to `O(N)`, and defer heavier wrapper allocations (like `DOMString::from`) until after uniqueness is guaranteed.
