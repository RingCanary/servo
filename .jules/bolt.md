## 2024-05-24 - Efficient Deduplication of DOM Collections
**Learning:** `Vec::contains()` and `Iterator::any()` result in O(N^2) complexity when used for deduplicating strings in tight loops within DOM collections (like `HTMLCollection`, `HTMLFormElement`, `NamedNodeMap`). `Atom` represents an interned string in Servo's script component, making its `clone()` operation extremely cheap (refcount increment). Using `HashSet<Atom>` or `HashSet<&str>` drops this to O(N).
**Action:** When deduplicating strings in `components/script`, use a `HashSet`. If the source is an `Atom`, insert the cloned `Atom` into the HashSet. Defer heavy `DOMString::from` allocations until uniqueness is confirmed. Avoid `.to_string().is_empty()` and instead call `.is_empty()` directly on the `Atom` or `&str`.
